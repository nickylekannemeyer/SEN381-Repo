Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 | POPIA Compliant

1. Information We Collect

In compliance with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), we collect only necessary user data, including your full name, email address, phone number, and location details associated with submitted service requests.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used exclusively for processing, tracking, and resolving municipal service requests within Mossel Bay, as well as sending automated status updates regarding your reported issues.

3. Data Security & Storage

All personal data is securely stored using encrypted database protocols (PostgreSQL and Prisma ORM) with restricted access limited to authorized municipal personnel.

4. Your Rights

You maintain the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time through your account settings or by contacting municipal support.
