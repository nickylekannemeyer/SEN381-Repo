2. Purpose of the Portal

CivicConnect is a municipal service request management system designed for residents of Mossel Bay to report public infrastructure issues (such as potholes, water leaks, and street lighting) and track resolution workflows.

3. User Accounts & Responsibilities

You are responsible for safeguarding your account credentials. You agree to submit accurate, truthful information and legitimate media evidence when filing service requests. False or malicious reporting may result in immediate account termination.

4. Service Level Disclaimers

While CivicConnect facilitates streamlined communication between citizens and municipal departments, operational response and resolution times are managed directly by Mossel Bay municipal teams and are subject to resource availability.
